Add / edit person — 4-step wizard (new screen) Replaces the flat single-screen "Add person" form with a linear wizard: Basic identity → Classification & location → Family & relationships → Notes. Same Input, Select-style trigger, Button, Chip, Avatar and IconButton chrome as the rest of the app. The step indicator, the repeater rows, the cascading Group→Subgroup and Governorate→City→Neighborhood pickers, the inline "+ Add new…" option and the person-search autocomplete are new composed patterns — see the delivery notes for details. Try it: fill step 1, hit Next. On step 2, pick a Group to unlock Subgroup, then a Governorate to unlock City, then a City to unlock Neighborhood — each picker has a "+ Add new" row at the bottom. On step 3, remove a row or add one. On step 4, type in Notes and watch the counter.
